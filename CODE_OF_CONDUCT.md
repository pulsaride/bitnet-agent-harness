# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in BitNet Agent Harness, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in technical discussions.
- Welcome questions and distinguish evidence, assumptions, and opinions.
- Accept feedback without personal attacks and correct mistakes transparently.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, accessibility needs, and differing experience levels.
- Credit others' work and comply with applicable licenses.
- Report security concerns through the [security policy](SECURITY.md), not through hostile disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, or sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately demeaning conduct.
- Publishing private personal information without explicit permission.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation of evidence, falsified benchmarks, or unauthorized disclosure.
- Encouraging or assisting abusive, illegal, or unsafe use of project capabilities.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project or interacts with its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to project maintainers through a private channel appropriate for the repository or the contact method established for the incident. Do not include unrelated private information or public accusations.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community. Responses will consider impact, context, safety, and available evidence, and will protect reporter privacy as far as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant, and is tailored to this experimental research project.
